# Responsible Use

FaceMatch performs facial recognition. Facial recognition can cause real harm
when misused. By using this software you agree to use it responsibly:

- **Only process images you own or are authorized to process** — your own photo
  library, an event you organized, or images of people who have consented.
- **Do not** use FaceMatch for mass surveillance, tracking individuals without
  consent, profiling, or identifying strangers from public or scraped imagery.
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations governing biometric data in
  your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, BIPA, CCPA).

The authors provide this software for legitimate personal and organizational
photo-management use and accept no liability for misuse.
